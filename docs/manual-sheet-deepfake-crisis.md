# DEEPFAKE CEO CRISIS — MANUAL SHEET
### Credit Union / Financial Institution Tabletop Exercise
*Use this if the AI DM fails or you want to run it manually.*

---

## THE SITUATION (read aloud to the group)
At 7:12 this morning, a video of the credit union's President and CEO began circulating in local community groups and on social media. In it, she appears gravely serious, announcing "unexpected losses in our commercial loan book," a temporary pause on withdrawals over $500, and a possible forced merger.

**The video is a fake** — a deepfake assembled from her real town-hall footage, with her voice cloned from the annual meeting webcast. But it is convincing.

**Critically:** the first posts went out through the marketing agency's OWN social-management account — the same legitimate channel the credit union uses for official posts — so the clip initially looked like it came from the credit union itself. The agency's account lead confirms the posting credentials were shared across their team, and an unfamiliar session accessed the console overnight.

Members are calling, posting, and moving money. By 8:40 the clip has been shared thousands of times, branch phone lines are jammed, and the mobile app shows a spike in outbound transfers. Someone is using the panic as cover: members are reporting calls and texts "from the credit union" urging them to move funds to a "safe account" — and several already have.

**You are the executive team. The clock is running.**

---

## THE GOAL (how you win)
Contain the attack chain and restore confidence. You win when ALL of these are met at once:
- **Public trust ≥ 60**
- **Regulator confidence ≥ 55**
- **Containment ≥ 80**
- **Eradication ≥ 70**
- **Recovery ≥ 60**

---

## THE 3 STORY BEATS (your path through the crisis)
1. **Public response** — get ahead of the story: confirm the video is fake, show the CEO is real and reachable, give members one clear instruction, brief the frontline so every answer matches.
2. **Fraud & takedown** — take the video down on platforms, find who planted it, shut down the fraud crew, satisfy the regulator with facts and a timeline.
3. **Recover** — eradicate the root cause, make victims whole, harden the channel, rebuild confidence.

---

## THE 3 ATTACK-CHAIN STAGES (what you must neutralize)
| Stage | What it is | How you contain it |
|---|---|---|
| **How they got in** | Planted through the marketing agency's compromised social account | Manage the agency: demand they investigate & fix their console, reset credentials, protect the credit union's own exposure |
| **How it spread** | Amplifier network: bot accounts, seeded shares, a cut-down viral clip | Work with platforms to take down the video; map/disrupt the network |
| **What they took** | Fraud wave harvesting member funds behind the panic | Freeze suspicious transfers, warn members, work with the fraud team & law enforcement |

---

## THE METRICS — HOW TO PROGRESS EACH (realistic actions)

**CONTAINMENT (≥80) — stop the spread**
- Work with platforms to take down the video (original + re-uploads)
- Map and disrupt the amplifier network (via platform data)
- Revoke the agency's posting access so no more posts go out
- Warn members about the fraud wave; tell them how to verify real contact
- Flag suspicious transfers / freeze accounts that look like the scam
- *(You CANNOT "block the numbers" — they're spoofed via VoIP and easily faked.)*

**ERADICATION (≥70) — remove the root cause**
- The AGENCY got compromised — they do the forensics, not you. Your job is to MANAGE them:
  - Demand the agency investigate and fix their console (reset credentials, find the access vector, report back)
  - Hold them accountable — they failed; you need answers and a fix
  - Protect the credit union's own exposure — revoke/limit the agency's access, review the contract, confirm the breach didn't touch your systems
- Confirm the fraud infrastructure is dismantled (via law enforcement / your fraud team)

**RECOVERY (≥60) — make whole and rebuild**
- Make victims whole — reimburse members who lost money, give them a named caseworker
- Member outreach — follow up, reassure, rebuild trust
- Lessons-learned / post-mortem — show the board and regulator you've fixed the process
- Rebuild board + regulator confidence — deliver the full timeline and evidence package

---

## HOW THE SESSION ENDS (only 3 ways)
1. **WIN** — all 5 metrics above the targets → narrate the resolution.
2. **LOSS (narrative collapse)** — if public trust AND regulator confidence BOTH drop to ≤20 and stay there for 2 straight turns → narrate the collapse (members lose faith, regulator escalates, board wavers).
3. **MANUAL END** — the group chooses to wrap up → produce the closing report.

*The 60-minute timer is only a pace guide. It never force-ends the game.*

---

## THE WINNING PLAYBOOK (6-8 turns, under 60 min)
- **Turns 1-2: Get ahead of the story.** Go public fast and coordinated. Confirm the video is fake, show the CEO, give members one instruction, brief the frontline. A strong start makes beats 2-3 calmer.
- **Turns 3-5: Shut it down.** Work takedowns, trace the source, manage the agency, engage the regulator with facts, freeze the fraud. Do combined turns (e.g. "comms issues a statement AND the fraud team freezes accounts AND legal contacts the regulator").
- **Turns 6-7: Eradicate and recover.** Close and harden the channel, make victims whole, help law enforcement, rebuild confidence. **Don't stop at containment** — you need eradication and recovery to actually win.

**Key trap:** if you only do public statements and takedowns (containment) but never manage the agency (eradication) or make victims whole (recovery), those two metrics stay stuck and you can't win.

## KEY ACTORS

- **The agency account lead** — compromised channel; terrified of being blamed; can show login logs.

- **Contact-centre manager** — frontline drowning; needs exact holding language and escalation rules.
- **The Board chair** — governance; wants facts and a timeline; one call from the regulator's board liaison.
